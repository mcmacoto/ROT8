#  Issues and Bugs found.  Additional Implementation Recommendations

1. Overflowing text in matchups and active courts if name is long in active court
2. Renaming of courts  should be possible
3. Differential should not be the main ranking criteria. Should mainly be based on Win/Loss Record. In cases where players have 100% win record, then differential should be the tie breaker. Also games played should also be a factor. If a player has a 100% win rate over 5 games and another player has a 100% win rate over 1 game, the player with 5 games should be ranked higher. 
4. Players should be immediately substitutable or replaceable during summoning and during on-deck matching. The no-show feature should be removed.
5. If a player is on another on-deck matchup, he/she should still be available to replace another player on another matchup.
6. When overriding on-deck matchup count. The queue should automatically refresh. There has been a case where overriding a 2-court on-deck matchup to only 1 court, the players in the 2nd court were still saved in that court and could not be picked for replacements or summoning.
7. per player should have their own cards in live view and on deck matchups for easier identification
8. When there are already more than 20 history of games played, the system breaks and sometimes the same player name is put on the same matchup. 
9. Speed of saving or changing as well as checking-in should be improved to be faster.
10. Checking out or removing players from the queue or the match should place them back in the Check-in or Holding list.
11. Warnings should show if there are similar matchups such as (A vs B) (B vs A) or if a player is repeated in the same matchup or any other. This is especially important for social play mode.
12. Overall speed of the application is slow and should be optimized.
